How this map is built

Every year, link and label on this map is traced to a published source. Estimates are marked, not guessed at.

Order & dates

Chronological order follows the MCU Complete Timeline published by Marvel / Disney+, covering feature films, Disney+ seasons, Marvel Studios One-Shots, specials and the animated titles Marvel currently counts.

In-universe years come from Marvel's own The Marvel Cinematic Universe: An Official Timeline (2023) or from dates stated on screen. Where the year is only a fan/press consensus, the card is marked ≈ and the sidebar says so.

Release order uses theatrical and streaming premiere dates. Titles from 2026 onward are scheduled dates and may have shifted.

What is never invented

Summaries describe only what happens on screen. Announced titles with no released material carry ⧗ and deliberately have no plot summary.

Connections in gold, blue, purple and pink are script facts — a direct continuation, a post-credits setup, a character crossing over, a multiverse branch. Grey dashed links are announced or inferred and are kept visually separate on purpose.

Known disputes, flagged on the cards

Placement of Eternals and Black Widow; the "8 years" line in Spider-Man: Homecoming that contradicts the official chronology; Marvel Television shows such as Agent Carter; and titles that sit off the main axis — Loki/TVA, What If…?, Marvel Zombies, and The Fantastic Four: First Steps on its own Earth.

Sources

  • Marvel.com / Disney+ — MCU Complete Timeline viewing guide
  • ScreenRant — MCU chronological order with the years from Marvel's official 2023 timeline book
  • Rotten Tomatoes Editorial and Vandal — chronological order, updated 2026
  • GamesRadar / TechRadar — confirmed anchors (2012, 2018, 2023) and ambiguous placements
  • Structure cross-checked against the Marvel Story Map (DeviantArt), the multiverse infographic covered by ScreenRant, Lucen Software's phase chart, Letterboxd watch-order lists and the MCU Fandom timeline

Out of scope

Comics are intentionally excluded: a trustworthy comics chronology needs its own dataset (event + tie-in model), not a single line.
